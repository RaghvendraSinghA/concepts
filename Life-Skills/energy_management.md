**What are some ideas that you can implement to sleep better?**

* Maintain a consistent sleep and wake-up time.
* Get enough sleep every night.
* Avoid using phones and laptops close to bedtime.
* Avoid caffeine late in the day.
* Exercise regularly.
* Keep the bedroom dark, quiet and comfortable.
* Avoid heavy meals just before sleeping.
* Follow a relaxing bedtime routine.
* Get sufficient exposure to natural sunlight during the day.
* Avoid long naps during the day.
