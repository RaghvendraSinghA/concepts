# Good Practices for Software Development

## Question 1: Which point(s) were new to you?

- Ask questions during the meeting itself, not after : -> Generally, I ask question after meeting/group discussions.
- Prefer group channels over private : -> Because, more visibility (more eyes) leads to faster, better-informed help and accountability.     
  Previously, I use to DM people.
- Never let a call go to "missed" :-> Instead, pick up and say you'll call back in 5–10 minutes.   


## Question 2: Which area do you think you need to improve on? What are your ideas to make progress in that area?   

### Requirements gathering and asking questions during meeting - :    
- To improve, make live notes during discussions instead of relying on memory, write down and share requirements immediately.
  Ask questions during the meeting.Be alert in meeting.   

### Mindful communication - :  
- Work on writing out all your questions first and sending them as a single, well-organized message,    
and be mindful of your teammates' own workload and focus time.

### Deep work / focus
- Silence all non-work notifications, removing distracting apps from your home screen,       
using a blocking tool (like TimeLimit or Freedom) during work hours, and tracking your time to stay accountable.     
Make time-table everyday of everything with required time to perform So, I don't forget them or keep less time   
to complete them.
