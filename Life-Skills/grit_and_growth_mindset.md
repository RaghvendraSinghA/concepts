# Grit and Growth Mindset

## Question 1: What is Grit?

Grit is the psychological ability to stay committed to a long-term goal and continue working despite failures, difficulties, or slow progress.Grit can enhance you even if you are already talented.

## Question 2: Introduction to Growth Mindset

A growth mindset is the belief that our abilities and intelligence can improve through learning, practice, effort, and feedback. Skills are built not god-gifted.

## Question 3: What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?

Internal Locus of Control means believing that our actions, decisions, and efforts have a significant influence on the results.

The key point is that instead of focusing too much on things we cannot control, we should focus our energy on the things we can control. For example, we may not be able to control the difficulty of an interview but, we can control our preparation, effort, practice, attitude, and response to difficulties.

## Question 4: What are the key points mentioned by the speaker to build a Growth Mindset?

1. Believe that abilities and intelligence can be developed, no one is god-gifted.
2. Believe on your ability to figure-out things.
3. Question your assumptions.
4. See challenges as opportunities to learn and grow.
5. Honour the difficulty.Fight it like a soldier and try to overcome it so, you can
   make it easier for others.
6. Create curricular for your life about what you want to do and follow it.
7. World keep change so be adaptive.


## Question 5: What are your ideas for taking action and building a Growth Mindset?

I can take the following actions to build a growth mindset:

* I will focus on improving my skills instead of worrying about whether I am naturally talented.
* I will break difficult problems into smaller parts and solve them step by step.
* I will practice coding concepts regularly instead of only watching tutorials.
* I will review my mistakes and try to understand why they happened.
* I will avoid comparing my progress too much with others and focus on my own improvement.
* I will take responsibility for the things I can control, such as my effort, practice, preparation, and attitude.
* I will stay consistent even when progress feels slow.
* I will remember that becoming a good software developer is a continuous learning process, and I do not need to know everything immediately.
