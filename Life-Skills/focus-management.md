# Focus Management

## Question 1 : What is Deep Work?
Deep Work is a state in which a person works with full-focus and mental power to perform a task.

## Question 2 : According to the author, how can you do deep work properly?

- Remove distractions: Keep away from social media, unnecessary notifications, messages, and other interruptions.     
                             Try to work in the morning.
- Set a fixed time and place -: Create a routine where you regularly perform deep work.
- Work for a defined period -: Decide beforehand how long you will focus and work during that period without distractions.
- Set clear goals: Know exactly -: what you want to accomplish before starting.
- Create a shutdown routine -: At the end of the workday, stop thinking about work and give your mind time to recover.

## Question 3 : How can you implement the principles in your day-to-day life?

I can implement deep work principles by:

- Setting a specific time for daily to do deep work.
- Keeping my phone away and turning off unnecessary notifications.
- Planning the most important tasks before starting my day.
- Reviewing my progress at the end of the day and planning the next day's priorities.

## Question 4 : What are the dangers of social media, in brief?

- Distraction -: It interrupts concentration and makes it harder to do deep work.
- Procrastination -: Social media can become an easy way to avoid difficult, boring, or uncomfortable tasks.
- Mental fatigue -: Constant exposure to information and notifications can make the brain feel overloaded, feels like
  now you can't do anything today, you have used 100% of your brain quota.
- No-reward for economy -: Social media doesn't reward anything, even it makes you irrelevant to job-market like you will
  not learn new skills which is required in this economy to earn, because you were wasting time on social media.
- No time to use brain's diffusion mode -: It will take your time in which you should use your brain diffusion mode to become
  creative and clear on your thoughts and doubts.
