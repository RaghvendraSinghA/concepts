# Learning Process

## Question 1: What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in one line.

The **Feynman Technique** is a learning method in which you develop a deep understanding of a topic by explaining it in simple words as if you were teaching someone else.

## Question 2: In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?

The most interesting idea for me was that the brain uses two different modes of thinking: **focused mode** and **diffused mode**. When we are unable to solve a problem by concentrating intensely on it, taking a break and allowing our mind to relax can help us see the problem from a different perspective.

## Question 3: What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

* **Focused mode:** This is when we concentrate intensely on a specific problem or task and use familiar patterns of thinking to solve it.

* **Diffused mode:** This is a relaxed way of thinking in which the mind makes broader connections and can help us discover new ideas or solutions.

## Question 4: According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic?

1. Deconstruct the skill.
2. Learn enough to self-correct.
3. Remove barriers to practice.

* I will give my full attention to the task I am working on.
* I will keep my phone on silent and remove unnecessary notifications while studying or coding.
* I will avoid social media and other distractions during my work hours.
