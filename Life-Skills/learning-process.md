## Question 3: What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

* **Focused mode:** This is when we concentrate intensely on a specific problem or task and use thinking to solve it.

* **Diffused mode:** This is a relaxed way of thinking in which the mind makes broader connections and can help us discover new ideas.
